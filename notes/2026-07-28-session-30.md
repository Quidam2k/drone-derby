# Session — Phase 30: Repair economy (2026-07-28)

## What landed

The 1994 repair rule, end to end: at end of turn a robot on a **wrench**
or a **checkpoint flag** discards 1 damage and moves its archive there.
Repair runs **before respawn** (a robot destroyed this turn does not
repair) and **before `cleanUpCards`** (standing phase-28 decision), so a
register unlocked by dropping below its lock threshold releases its card
into the shared discard pile the same turn — next hand is one card bigger
with no new card-flow code, because `cleanUpCards` already discards every
effective card that is no longer locked.

## Shape of the change

- **Engine**: `TileDef` + `'wrench'`; new events `repair` /
  `register-unlocked` (mirror `damage` / `register-locked`);
  `repairRobots(ctx)` in `execute.ts` between the win check and
  `respawnRobots`; `validate.ts` accepts wrench (no payload, no numbering,
  not a hazard).
- **Boards**: 2 wrenches per built-in, placed off spawn rows and laser
  lines (see the layout sketches). The golden EventLog snapshot did NOT
  re-bake — verified, no fixed-program robot ends turn 1 on one.
- **Presentation**: visualState folds `repair` into the damage counter;
  captions "X repairs 1 damage (n/10)" / "X's register r unlocks!";
  durations 300/600 ms (same as their mirrors); audio maps repair to the
  existing `respawn` clip (no new audio this phase), unlock silent;
  director interest 0.35/0; scene gained `effects.repair()` — a
  checkpoint-green ring contracting onto the chassis.
- **Renderers**: DOM `WrenchSprite` (45° wrench on a green dashed hatch);
  3D `wrench` piece in `tiles.py` (service hatch + modelled wrench,
  `tiles.glb` re-exported via `npm run art:tiles`, CPU) with a
  pad-plus-glyph procedural fallback in `boardMesh.ts`; editor tool
  "Repair (wrench)".
- **Tests**: 338 green. New `repair.test.ts` covers wrench/flag repair,
  floor at 0 damage, pass-over-doesn't-count, dead-robot-no-repair,
  5→4 unlocking register 5 (freed card lands in the shared discard,
  conservation stays 84), 7→6 freeing only register 3, idle-lock unlock
  with `card: null`. Plus visualState fold test, caption/duration pins,
  audio exhaustiveness list extended.

## Gotchas for later phases

- `run()` in engine test helpers injects fabricated cards into hands
  WITHOUT removing their twins from the shared draw pile — fine unless a
  test asserts conservation. `repair.test.ts` has a `takeFromDeck` helper
  for that; reuse it in any future conservation-sensitive test.
- The wrench kit piece's local frame = its fallback primitive
  (`CylinderGeometry(0.36, 0.36, 0.04)` placed at `centre(x, y, 0.025)`).
  Keep both paths agreeing if the piece is remodelled.
