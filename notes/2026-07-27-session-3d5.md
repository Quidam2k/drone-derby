# Phase 3D-5 — the real camera director (2026-07-27)

Replaces "fly to the current event" with a director that decides what is worth
a shot, frames the whole of it, and holds still otherwise.

## What shipped

- **New `src/components/board3d/directorMath.ts`** (pure, no `three`, no DOM —
  the `viewMath.ts` / `effectMath.ts` pattern). `interestOf`, `eventPoints`,
  `lookaheadWindow`, `planShot`, `contains`, `shouldReplace`, `easeTau`.
  62 tests.
- **`camera.ts`**: takes a `Shot` instead of a bare `Position`; rim overhang;
  distance-adaptive ease; reduced-motion pin; `cuts()` / `heldSeconds()` /
  `currentShot()`. `eventFocus` deleted — generalised into `eventPoints`.
- **`scene.ts`**: `BoardSceneInput` gains `events?` + `cursor?`; `refocus()`
  plans over a look-ahead window; `view()` gains `shot` + `cuts`; `probe()`
  gains `screen`.
- **`Board.tsx`** gains two optional props it ignores; **`Board3D.tsx`**
  forwards them; **`ReplayPlayer.tsx`** passes them. That is the whole
  upstream footprint.
- New `directorTurn.test.ts` (the headline measurement, 12 tests) and
  `Board3D.props.test.ts` (5 tests, the 3D-4 dropped-prop tripwire).

## The decision it turns on

**The director looks ahead.** In a replay the whole log already exists, so the
camera can be framed on the laser *before* it fires. Without that, any director
is a chaser: at TAU 0.36 s it arrives as the event ends however good the
scoring is. Measured live — the camera sat on the `robot-fell` shot through
four preceding events (turn-started, register-started, two card reveals) and
was already still when "Brie falls off the board!" appeared.

## The two numbers the plan guessed wrong

Both were swept against real logs rather than argued about.

- **`MIN_DWELL` is 1.6 s, not the planned ~0.45 s.** Events average under half
  a second, so a half-second floor touches almost nothing: at 0.45 s a Grand
  Circuit turn still re-aims 43 times in 39 s. Clustering and containment were
  swept too and neither was the binding constraint. The dwell is. The cost is
  real and accepted: an event three tiles outside the current framing waits for
  the shot to expire, so the camera does sometimes miss one.
- **A board laser that hits nothing scores 0.15, not 0.5** — below
  `MIN_INTEREST`, so it cannot move the camera at all. Found by watching the
  instrumented replay, not by reasoning: Grand Circuit's laser row sits ten
  tiles north of where the robots race, and while board and robot lasers were
  scored alike the camera whipped off the robots and back four times a turn. A
  *player's* missed shot still rates 0.4. That one change took Grand Circuit
  from 188 re-aims to 96.

## The 3D-4 open question, closed

A robot thrown off the SOUTH rim used to leave the bottom of the canvas:
`focus()` clamped the subject to keep the framing over the board, and the
viewport has headroom only *above* it. `camera.ts` now allows a bounded 1.5
tiles of overhang, and `eventPoints` gives an edge fall a virtual point 1.3
tiles outside the rim so the shot reaches for it.

Measured through the whole throw (`probe().screen`, canvas 500×621): board y
16.20 → 17.05 (a full tile past the last row), height +0.10 → −2.90, screen y
488 → 561, `onScreen: true` on **every** frame. `screengrab/3d5-edge-fall.png`.

## Traps

- **A frame-ticked dwell clock is wrong here.** The obvious `held += dt` in
  `step()` breaks because the scene renders on demand: the rAF loop stops once
  the camera, rigs and effects settle, so the clock would run at 100% of real
  time on a board with animating conveyors and ~5% on one without. The camera
  would freeze on its first shot of the turn on exactly the boards where it is
  hardest to notice. It is a `performance.now()` timestamp instead.
- **The look-ahead window has to reach past five card reveals.**
  `register-started` + 5 × `card-revealed` is six events of nothing worth a
  shot; a shorter window frames the whole board through the 2.8 s register
  lead-in, which is the yank-out symptom the phase was fixing. `LOOKAHEAD` is
  10.
- **`contains` must test content against radius, not radius against radius.**
  Every single-cell shot asks for `MIN_RADIUS`, so a radius-to-radius test
  calls two cells three tiles apart "different framings" and re-aims for a cell
  that was already near the middle of the frame. Hence `Shot.content`.
- **`focus()` had to become idempotent, which broke `setBoard`.** Callers
  re-run the follow rule every update, so a dwell timer reset on every call is
  not a dwell timer — but the early-out then skips the re-centring a new
  board's dimensions need. `setBoard` sets the fields directly.
- **`planShot` admits the seed event's points unconditionally.** `MAX_SPREAD`
  is about not merging *unrelated* events; an eight-tile beam is one shot
  however wide, and capping it would frame the muzzle with the impact
  off-screen.
- **The MCP browser throttles rAF to ~1 Hz when the page is not focused**, and
  `MAX_DT` clamps `dt` to 0.05 s, so a 0.75 s fall animation takes 15 real
  seconds and reads as frozen. `page.bringToFront()` first, or every timing
  measurement is nonsense.
- **A stale service-worker precache serves the previous build's `index.html`**,
  which then 404s on the new scene chunk and `[board3d] failed to start`.
  Unregister the SW and clear caches after every rebuild before testing.

## Verified

`npm run typecheck` clean, **264 tests green**, production build. Browser
(preview build, Grand Circuit, 1280 and 375): the subject holds through every
`card-revealed` run and through `register-started` — the camera never goes wide
mid-turn; `follow=free` freezes the subject (0 cuts over 6 events);
`follow=robot` locks at `ROBOT_RADIUS` 4.6; `prefers-reduced-motion` pins the
whole-board framing with **zero** director movement over 30 events; zero
console errors.

## Gates

- **3D-4's gate is still open**: Todd watches a replay end to end for effect
  noise. Nothing here touches an effect.
- **This phase's gate**: does the camera read as *directed*? The numbers say it
  holds a shot for 2–3 seconds and stops chasing. Whether that feels
  cinematic or asleep is a judgement only watching can make. If it is too
  still, `MIN_DWELL` is the one knob; if it is still restless, `MIN_INTEREST`
  is the other.
