### Cross-phase decision confirmed by fix #3

`cleanUpCards` recomputes `stillLocked` from the **current**
`lockedRegisters`. So when phase 30 (repair) or 33 (power-down) lowers
damage and unlocks a register, the freed card returns to the discard pile
for free — **provided the repair/power-down step runs before
`cleanUpCards`**. That ordering is load-bearing, not incidental.

### New test: `src/engine/__tests__/invariants.test.ts`

Property test, 220 seeded random games × 40 turns (Proving Grounds 3p,
Spin Cycle 4p, solo), ~2s. Asserts per turn: 84-card conservation, no
duplicate ids, hand size == `handSize(damage)`, no card on an unlocked
register, damage/lives in range, no two live robots sharing a cell,
nobody resting in a pit or off-board, input state not mutated. Terminal
states check conservation only.

**Tripwire verified** — reverting the `execute.ts` fix fails all three
cases with "card conservation: expected 80 to be 84". This is the safety
net for phases 30–33.
