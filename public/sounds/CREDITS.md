# Sound Credits

All sound effects are from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) asset packs,
licensed **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain — no attribution required,
provided with thanks).

Phase 25 treatment: every clip is trimmed to a short length budget, given
a 10ms fade-in / 60ms fade-out, loudness-normalized to −23 LUFS
(true peak ≤ −2 dBFS; very short clips land quieter where the peak cap
binds), and encoded mono MP3 with ffmpeg for size + Safari compatibility.
